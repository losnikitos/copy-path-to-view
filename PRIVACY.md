# Privacy Policy for Copy path to view

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Copy path to view reads the page you right-click, copies a short description to your clipboard, and does not send that description anywhere.

## What data is used

When you right-click, the extension looks at the element under the cursor and nearby HTML comments. If you then choose **Copy path to view**, it copies this text to the clipboard:

- the address of the current page
- the nearest `BEGIN` view path, and up to two parent view paths
- a short path to the element, its attributes, and up to 80 characters of its visible text

## How data is stored

Nothing is saved. The extension has no account, no analytics, and no server. The text exists only in memory until you copy it, then on your clipboard. A toast on the page shows the same text for a few seconds and is then removed.

## How data is used

That text is the feature: it tells you which view rendered the element you clicked. It is not used for anything else.

## Third-party services

This extension does not use third-party services.

## Data sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or transmitted off your device by the extension. Pasting the clipboard text is up to you.

## Retention and deletion

There is nothing to delete inside the extension. Clear your clipboard if you do not want to keep the copied text. Uninstalling the extension removes it completely.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this URL with a new date.
